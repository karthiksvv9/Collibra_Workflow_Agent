Collibra Governed Data Access Workflow Deployment Guide

This document provides a concise, deployment-ready overview of the **Create A Complex Collibra Governed Data Access Workflow**. It covers purpose, route logic, forms, Groovy scripts, testing, risks, and operating procedures for business and platform users.

---

Purpose

Automate governed data access requests, triage, approvals, risk assessments, policy exceptions, provisioning, and notifications within Collibra. Ensures compliance, accountability, and streamlined access management.

---

Workflow Overview

  • Initiated by a user submitting a governed access request.
  • Sequential validation, triage, and decision points involving data stewards, business owners, and risk/compliance teams.
  • Conditional paths for approvals, rework, exceptions, and provisioning.
  • Final status updates and notifications upon completion or rejection.

---

Route Logic Summary

  • **Start**: Requester submits form → validation.
  • **Validation**: Checks request context and RAG mappings.
  • **Triage**: Data steward reviews; approves or requests rework.
  • **Risk & Compliance**: High risk or restricted data routes to review.
  • **Business Approval**: Business owner approves or requests rework.
  • **Policy Exception**: If needed, logged and created.
  • **Relations & Provisioning**: Relations established; downstream provisioning invoked.
  • **Outcome**: Asset status updated; notifications sent; workflow ends.

---

| Rework Request | Clarify or modify request | 3 fields |

| Steward Triage | Data steward review | 3 fields |

| Business Approval | Business owner decision | 2 fields |

| Risk & Compliance Review | Risk assessment | 3 fields |

| Technical Remediation | Fix provisioning issues | 3 fields |

---

Groovy Scripts

  • **Validation**: Ensures request integrity and RAG mappings.
  • **Create Policy Exception**: Logs exception metadata if needed.
  • **Create Relations**: Establishes data responsibilities.
  • **Update Access Status**: Sets asset status and logs audit trail.
  • **Notifications**: Sends success or rejection alerts.

*Note:* Scripts are optimized for deployment; review for environment-specific adjustments.

- Compile all Groovy scripts and sequence listeners.

  • **Functional Testing**:

  • **Regression Testing**:

Risks & Mitigation

- Validate form configurations and permissions.

2. **Deployment**:

- Import the BPMN package into Collibra.

4. **Go-Live**:

5. **Maintenance**:

- Regularly review logs and audit trails.

This workflow automates complex data access governance processes, ensuring compliance, transparency, and efficiency. Proper testing and validation are critical prior to deployment. Follow operating procedures for smooth rollout and ongoing management.
