## Purpose
Automates the end-to-end process for data access requests, including validation, triage, risk assessment, approvals, provisioning, and notifications, ensuring compliance and efficiency.

---

## Workflow Overview
- **Start:** Requester initiates access request.
- **Validation:** Request is validated; rework loop if invalid.
- **Triage & Decision:** Data steward assesses request; risk and policy review determine routing.
- **Approvals:** Business owner and security teams approve or reject.
- **Provisioning:** Approved requests trigger data access provisioning.
- **Notifications:** Success or rejection notifications are sent.
- **End:** Workflow concludes with approval or rejection.

---

## Route Logic & Sequence Flows
- **Request Initiation:** `start_request` → `submit_request`
- **Validation:** `submit_request` → `validate_request`
- **Validation Outcome:**
  - Fail: `validate_request` → `requester_rework` → back to validation
  - Pass: `validate_request` → `steward_triage`
- **Triage & Decision:**
  - Triage: `steward_triage` → `gateway_steward_decision`
  - Decision: `gateway_steward_decision` → risk review (`gateway_risk_route`)
- **Risk & Policy Review:**
  - Low risk or exception: `business_approval`
  - High risk: `security_review`
- **Approvals & Provisioning:**
  - Business approval: `business_approval` → provisioning (`call_provisioning_workflow`)
  - Policy exception: `create_policy_exception` → provisioning
- **Provisioning Outcome:**
  - Success: `provisioning_result` → success notification
  - Failure: `provisioning_result` → rejection notification
- **Rejections:** Triggered at business or compliance decision points, leading to rejection notifications.
- **Workflow End:** With either approval (`end_approved`) or rejection (`end_rejected`).
